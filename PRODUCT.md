# eyemouse (consumer app)

Consumer packaging of OpenGaze: a macOS menu bar app that moves the real pointer wherever someone looks and clicks when they blink.

## Who
- **The person using it:** limited hand or arm mobility. Uses gaze, blinks and winks; may or may not be able to speak.
- **The person setting it up:** usually a helper or caregiver with a mouse. They grant permissions and position the camera, then hand over for calibration and practice.

## Scope (v1)
- Gaze cursor with blink click, system-wide
- Voice bar: dictate into any app (⌃⌥D), read selection aloud (⌃⌥S)
- Gaze keyboard
- Practice game

## Setup surface
Minimal first-run flow that gets from install to a working pointer: camera, Accessibility (via System Settings), optional microphone, camera positioning, 9-dot calibration, a first blink-click, then the menu bar.

## Brand
Wink logo: lime rounded square (#CCF95E), electric violet (#5512F0) face and wordmark, warm off-white ground (#FBF7EE). Chunky rounded lowercase wordmark. Friendly, plain-spoken, never clinical.

## Constraints
- Not a medically validated device; don't claim accuracy it doesn't have.
- Video is processed on the Mac. Voice uses Cartesia in the cloud, only while dictating or speaking.
- Big targets everywhere: the user may be clicking by blink.
